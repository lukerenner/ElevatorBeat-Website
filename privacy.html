Privacy Policy

Last updated July 28, 2026. Applies to elevatorbeat.com, Dragify, and StraightPic.

Elevator Beat LLC (“Elevator Beat,” “we,” “us”) builds Dragify and StraightPic. This policy explains what information our apps and website collect, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Photos you provide. Dragify and StraightPic process the images you choose to edit. Photos are used only to generate the result you requested and are not used to train models without your separate, explicit consent.
  • Account and purchase data. If you subscribe through the App Store, Apple shares limited transaction information (such as purchase status) needed to unlock features. We do not receive your payment card details.
  • Device and diagnostic data. Basic crash and performance data may be collected to keep the apps stable, consistent with Apple’s platform privacy controls.
  • Contact information. If you email us for support, we keep that correspondence to respond to you and improve the apps.

How we use information

We use the information above to operate the apps, process the specific edit you request, provide support, and maintain the security and reliability of our services. We do not sell your personal information.

Photo retention

Images processed by Dragify and StraightPic are used to generate your result and are deleted from our processing systems shortly after the result is returned, except where retained briefly for troubleshooting a reported issue.

Third parties

We use trusted infrastructure and AI-processing providers to run image generation and hosting. These providers process data on our behalf under contractual confidentiality and data-protection terms, and do not use your content for their own purposes.

Your choices

You can stop using an app at any time, request deletion of data associated with your support correspondence, or ask what information we hold about you by contacting us below.

Children’s privacy

Our apps are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our apps evolve. Material changes will be reflected by an updated “last updated” date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@elevatorbeat.com.
