Dragify support

Dragify transforms a selfie into a collection of original drag looks. If a generation fails, stalls, or the result doesn’t look right, try again with a clearer, front-facing photo and good lighting — most issues come down to source image quality.

  • Trouble with a purchase or subscription: contact us with your App Store order number.
  • A style didn’t generate correctly: tell us which style and we’ll take a look.
  • Account or data questions: see the privacy policy.

StraightPic support

StraightPic corrects perspective in photos of buildings, artwork, and documents. StraightPic is not yet available — support requests will be answered once the app ships.

Still need help?

Email hello@elevatorbeat.com with the app name, a short description of the issue, and your device type. We read every message and typically reply within two business days.
