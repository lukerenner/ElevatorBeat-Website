The studio

One clear job. Done well.

Most software keeps growing until the original idea disappears. ElevatorBeat starts with a specific frustration, builds the smallest useful answer, and gives that answer the care of a much larger product.

We design, build and ship everything ourselves from Portland, Oregon — which is why there are two apps here and not twenty.

  • Start narrow

    If the problem takes more than a sentence to describe, it is more than one problem, and we have not finished thinking.

  • Finish it

    A settings screen is usually a decision we failed to make. We would rather make the decision and be judged on it.

  • Get out of the way

    An app that has done its job should end. Ours are built to hand the work back and leave you alone.

StraightPic · Photography

Fix the perspective. Keep the photograph.

Buildings, artwork, documents — anything shot at an angle comes out leaning. Draw along the lines that should be straight. StraightPic corrects the perspective, reframes the image and removes the empty edges the correction leaves behind.
