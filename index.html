About Elevator Beat

Built in Portland. Made for creative work.

We work across product design, software development, image processing, and generative AI. Each app begins with one clear job and gets only the features it needs to do that job well. We care about how the product looks, but also how quickly someone understands it. The interaction should feel obvious. The result should feel worth keeping. Elevator Beat is still new. Dragify and StraightPic are the beginning.

How we build

Start narrow. Finish properly.

  1. We choose problems that can support a focused app rather than a sprawling platform.
  2. We spend time on the parts users notice immediately: the first screen, the central interaction, the quality of the output, and the point where the app either earns its place or gets deleted.
  3. When the idea is complete, we ship it. Then we move to the next one.

Elevator Beat

A small machine that takes an idea somewhere else.

The name comes from the rhythm of making things: enter with one version of an idea, press the button, and arrive with another. Some trips are practical. Some are strange. The useful ones change your point of view.
