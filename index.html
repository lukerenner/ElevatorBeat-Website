Portland, Oregon · Creative App Studio

We make apps for people who make things.

ElevatorBeat is an independent app studio in Portland, Oregon. We build focused creative tools around ideas we want to see exist — working across product design, software development, image processing, and generative AI.

Each app begins with one clear job and gets only the features it needs to do that job well. The interaction should feel obvious. The result should feel worth keeping. ElevatorBeat is still new: Dragify and StraightPic are the beginning.

Our approach

Small ideas can become very good apps.

Most software starts with a market category. Ours usually starts with something more specific: a photograph that should be easy to fix, a visual idea that would be fun to explore, or a tool we keep looking for and cannot find. We design and build the answer ourselves, then keep working until it feels simple.

Meet the first two releases from ElevatorBeat.

Dragify · AI Portraits

See yourself in drag.

Dragify transforms a selfie into a collection of original drag looks. Choose a style and let the app reinterpret your face through makeup, hair, fashion, lighting, and character. The result still looks like you—just with a much larger entrance.

StraightPic · Photography

Fix the perspective. Keep the photograph.

StraightPic corrects photos of buildings, artwork, documents, and other subjects that look skewed because they were taken at an angle. Draw along the lines that should be straight. The app adjusts the perspective, reframes the image, and removes the empty edges created by the correction.
