The studio

We built the apps we could not find.

StraightPic started with a photograph of a smokestack that looked like it was falling over. Dragify started because we wanted to see ourselves in drag.

Both apps are designed, drawn, built and supported by the same people, in Portland.

01/Dragify

Dragify

PurposeDrag portraits from one photo
PlatformiPhone
StatusOn the App Store
PriceFree, packs $3.99

Turn one photograph into a drag portrait. Choose a look, add a photo and Dragify creates a full-resolution image that keeps you recognizable.

There are around ninety looks, in five collections.

02/StraightPic

StraightPic

PurposePerspective correction
PlatformiPhone
StatusComing soon
PriceFree

StraightPic fixes the perspective distortion that makes buildings lean in photographs. Mark the lines that should be straight and the app corrects and reframes the image.
