ElevatorBeat

A Portland digital art collective.

We all have day jobs. But we also have Claude. And we believe that if we don’t tap into this highly creative time, we’ll go nuts — so we built this digital studio, a space we can inhabit between Slack messages.

Right now our explorations touch the iPhone (sorry, Android) but that’s not all we’re capable of. This space will come to curate our other tinkerings across digital, narrative, and the cinematic arts.

The studio

Software as story.

We have always wanted to make apps but never had the patience to learn how to code. The age of AI has unlocked a new level of creativity for us. We sketch, prototype, build, and release apps with the care we would give any other creative work.

Some begin with a problem we want solved (the default image straightener is weak). Others begin with an image, an interaction, or a question that must be answered (what would I look like as a drag queen).

Each one becomes its own small world — and, to carry the metaphor of this page forward, a room.

From idea to experiment to launch.

The Gallery

Dragify

An imagemaking app for drag portraits that couldn’t exist any other way.

Artist statement:

I built Dragify for two reasons. The first is I wanted to see what I would look like transformed into a drag queen. I also wanted to see what was possible in terms of AI-generated fashion.

Turns out, entire catalogues can be built in a flash. What started as a pool of more than 5,000 images was eventually pared down to the handful of curated collections you’ll find in the app.

The study

StraightPic

A gay little tool for making crooked photos straight again.

Artist statement:

My husband is an architect and I always have to ask him to square up and straighten my photos because I can never do it and the default iPhone tools are piss poor.

And so, we built this app to be exactly what we wanted out of a straightener: simple UI, precise controls, and 0% quality loss when you save.

We thought it would be kind of funny to style this app that’s all about being straight, and make it a little gay.
